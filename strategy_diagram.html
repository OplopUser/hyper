EMA Crossover

Fast EMA (9) reacts quickly to price.
Slow EMA (21) shows the trend.

🟢 Fast crosses above Slow → Long
🔴 Fast crosses below Slow → Short

Checked on the last closed candle; each closed candle can signal only once.

RSI Filter (14)

Prevents chasing overextended moves.

RSI < 30 = oversold → don't short
RSI > 70 = overbought → don't long
30–70 = neutral zone → trade OK

Keeps the bot out of exhausted trends.

ATR (14) — SL & TP

Sizes stop-loss & take-profit to current volatility.

Stop-loss = 1.5 × ATR from the signal close
Take-profit = 3.0 × ATR from the signal close

Reward : risk 2 : 1 from that close (entry is the mid-price, so it varies slightly).
Wide markets get wider stops automatically.

4h Trend Filter

4h close vs 4h EMA(21).

Above → bullish: shorts blocked
Below → bearish: longs blocked
Too little 4h data → unknown: nothing blocked

Volatility Filter

Current ATR ranked against the last 100 ATR values.

Below 20th percentile → ranging, skip
Above 95th percentile → news / chaos, skip
In between → trade OK

Position Sizing

Formula:
Size = (Equity × 2%) ÷ |Entry − Stop Loss|

Equity $1,000, ETH at $2,000, SL $50 away:
Size = $20 ÷ $50 = 0.4 ETH ($800 notional)

Loss at the stop: $20 (2% of equity) at any leverage — leverage only lowers the margin used. Size is capped at free margin × leverage × 95%, so very tight stops risk less.

Safety Rules

🔴 Max 3 open positions at once
🔴 10% drawdown halts all new trades
🔴 Only 1 position per coin at a time
🔴 Minimum order $10 notional
🔴 Restart keeps positions and re-adopts them (CLOSE_ON_SHUTDOWN=true closes instead)
